Welcome to Aegis Academy, Tier Three: Boardroom Decision Discipline.

This tier is gated. You are here because you are a leader who makes consequential decisions, and because the discipline you carry at the board level shapes how dozens or hundreds of other people make theirs.

Tier Three is the unique Aegis intellectual property. The seven A.I.-decision pathologies. Most "A.I. for executives" content teaches general decision-making with A.I. as a sidecar. Aegis names what is new about decision-making in the A.I. era and the discipline that addresses it.

Pathology one: A.I.-drafted analysis presented as analysis. Pathology two: A.I. consensus pressure. Pathology three: vendor capture via demos. Pathology four: optionality bloat. Pathology five: compounding error in multi-step planning. Pathology six: hallucinated citations. Pathology seven: A.I.-mediated information asymmetry between board and operators.

You will pair each pathology with one of the seven executive lenses. C.E.O. carries optionality. C.F.O. carries A.I.-as-analysis. C.M.O. carries information asymmetry. C.T.O. carries vendor capture. C.O.O. carries compounding error. C.P.O. carries consensus pressure. C.I.S.O. carries hallucinated citations.

You will write the reversibility check, the unwind plan, the stakeholder coalition map, the named-owner attestation, the four-axis risk score, and the governance posture for the decision domain. You will compose all of it into the Boardroom Decision Review Packet.
