Welcome to Aegis Academy, Tier One: Operating Discipline.

You are here because you are ready to own at least one A.I.-assisted workflow inside your job. Not personal productivity. Not experiments. A real workflow that other people depend on.

Tier One teaches the five questions to ask before you point A.I. at a workflow. It teaches context as inventory, context as scaffolding, and context as memory. It teaches the eval ritual: a daily fifteen-minute discipline that tells you whether your workflow is getting better or worse over time.

You will write kill criteria, so that the workflow either survives honestly or fails honestly. You will learn the data classification triage, so that nothing private leaks into a public tool. You will learn what drift looks like and how to catch it before it becomes a customer incident.

The capstone is a twelve-section Workflow Operating Plan. You will run it for fourteen days. Your manager will read it and either sign or send it back with specific fixes.

If you complete Tier One, you carry the Aegis Operator credential at the practitioner level. That is a real signal: you supervise A.I.-assisted work with discipline, and you can prove it. Ready?
